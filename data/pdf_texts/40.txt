Two Ways to Reach an Answer
Businesses don’t run on probability.
HOW AN LLM WORKS
Runs on probability
Predicts the next most-probable token. The output is fluent 
and often impressive — “that’s powerful” — but it is a 
probability distribution, not a guarantee.
When the cost of being wrong is high:
Fluent Guessing
isn’t good enough.
HOW A BUSINESS RUNS
A deterministic chain

2

3
Constraints   what cannot happen
4
Consequences   what follows

how the business works, where information lives, what rules govern decisions.
AI agents lack this implicit context — they see data but not meaning.

• No business rules or constraints
• No relationship semantics
• No governed metric definitions

ungovernable outcomes

What a formal ontology really is
A formal ontology is a mathematically precise 
model of a business domain that a machine can 
reason over. It is defined using formal logic with 
precise semantics and rigorous rules, commonly 
represented with standards such as OWL on RDF.
It is not a glossary or a tag list. It states facts such 
as “every laptop is a computer” and “every 
computer is electronics,” so a system can follow 
the chain and draw conclusions.
Classes, relationships, and rules a machine can follow.
