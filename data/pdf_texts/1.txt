=== Page 30 ===
Toy Preorder Lottery Service
You are the system vendor for an agent. The agent already has a real-name veriﬁcation 
app where buyers log in and complete real-name veriﬁcation, and it already has a store 
system. The agent wants to run toy preorders at the lowest possible cost:
●
Operators set each preorder campaign's period and the total number of toys each 
buyer may enter for;
●
Buyers register for the items they want to enter during the campaign period;
●
When the campaign closes, a lottery picks the winners;
●
Winners choose which store they want to pick their goods up from.
Our job is to plug all of this into the existing real-name app, not to build a separate 
buyer app, and not to rebuild the store system.
